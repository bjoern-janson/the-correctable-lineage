## Interpretation boundary

A positive result establishes only that a paid, predeclared sensor can restore a target-relevant distinction erased by the initial interface, and that selective acquisition can outperform model-only updating after cost.

It does not establish autonomous sensor invention, unknown-variable discovery, open-ended ontology generation, or a theory beyond existing active sensing, costly feature acquisition, or POMDP formalisms.
