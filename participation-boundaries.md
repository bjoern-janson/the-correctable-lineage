# Research Participation Boundaries

## Purpose

This document defines conditions for responsible engagement with the corpus.

The purpose is not to prohibit criticism, disagreement, or attempted falsification. The purpose is to preserve the integrity of the research object by preventing distortion, authority spillover, unsupported interpretation, and failure to distinguish claims from evidence.

## Participation principle

Responsible engagement with the corpus requires:

* accurately reconstructing the current claim boundary;
* distinguishing hypotheses, interpretations, and established results;
* preserving uncertainty, provenance, and reopening conditions;
* criticizing mechanisms, representations, or evidence rather than dismissing the research object without analysis;
* evaluating claims independently from personal preference or initial impression.

## External engagement records

This section records unsuccessful engagement attempts when they reveal potential failure modes relevant to corpus communication or evaluation.

The purpose of these records is not to classify individuals permanently. The unit of analysis is the interaction pattern and the failure mode observed.

Evidence status must remain explicit.

## Reopening condition

No external engagement record is permanent.

A record may be revised if additional evidence demonstrates:

* accurate reconstruction of the corpus;
* substantive engagement with claims and mechanisms;
* good-faith criticism;
* willingness to update when presented with counterevidence.

The purpose of this document is not exclusion. It is maintaining a traceable history of interaction patterns that affect interpretation, evaluation, and communication.
