# Research Participation Boundaries

## Purpose

This document defines conditions for responsible engagement with the corpus.

The purpose is not to prohibit criticism, disagreement, or attempted falsification. The purpose is to prevent misuse of corpus concepts through distortion, authority spillover, or bad-faith reconstruction.

## Participation principle

Engagement with the corpus requires:

* accurately representing the current claim boundary;
* distinguishing hypotheses from established results;
* preserving uncertainty and provenance;
* criticizing mechanisms rather than dismissing or misrepresenting the research object;
* allowing claims to be evaluated independently from personal preference.

Individuals may be recorded here when their documented engagement demonstrates patterns incompatible with responsible corpus use.

The relevant unit is the failure mode, not the person.

## Reopening condition

No person-level status is permanent.

A previous failure record may be revised if future interaction demonstrates:

* accurate reconstruction of the corpus;
* good-faith criticism;
* separation of personal reaction from evidential assessment;
* willingness to update when confronted with counterevidence.

The goal is not exclusion. The goal is preserving the integrity of the research object.
