# External Review Log

## Purpose

This file records external corrections to the research program.

It is not a testimonial page, endorsement list, or authority-by-affiliation mechanism.

A review earns influence only through the specificity of the evidence, argument, duplication claim, comparator correction, case-design criticism, or execution constraint it exposes.

The log should preserve:

- what question was asked;
- what kind of expertise or perspective responded;
- what correction was proposed;
- which project claim or design element changed;
- which claims remained unresolved;
- whether the outcome was absorption, narrowing, rejection, or no update.

---

## Privacy and attribution

Private correspondence should not be published verbatim or attributed by name without explicit permission.

Each entry records an attribution status:

- **public** — the reviewer and response may be named or linked;
- **anonymized** — role and substantive correction may be recorded, but identity is withheld;
- **private** — retained outside the public repository;
- **permission pending** — anonymized until explicit attribution permission is obtained.

Institutional prestige does not increase the evidential weight of an entry by itself.

---

## Classification vocabulary

Each correction may receive one or more classifications:

- **absorption** — mature practice already contains the proposed object or behavior;
- **terminology correction** — established terms should replace or constrain private vocabulary;
- **claim-boundary change** — a claim must be weakened, removed, split, or rescoped;
- **comparator correction** — an alternative was represented below native strength;
- **case-design change** — a test leaks the intended ontology or underdetermines the target behavior;
- **scoring change** — the outcome measure rewards the framework by construction;
- **execution constraint** — usability, ethics, recruitment, tooling, or governance blocks execution;
- **distinct testable residue** — a concrete difference remains after comparison;
- **unresolved** — the response identifies a live question but does not discriminate it;
- **rejection** — the proposed contribution should not continue in its current form;
- **no update** — the response does not materially alter a claim or evaluation design.

## Incorporation rule

External feedback should change the repository only when it modifies at least one of:

- the claim boundary;
- literature placement;
- comparator fidelity;
- case neutrality;
- scoring validity;
- execution feasibility;
- privacy, ethics, or evaluation governance.

Praise, broad interest, and conceptual resonance do not by themselves justify new structure.

The governing rule for this log is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Record the correction path, not the prestige of the corrector.}
}
\]
