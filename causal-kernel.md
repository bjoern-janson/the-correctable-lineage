# Causal Kernel

## Status

This file compresses the recurring causal claims of the corpus.

It is a synthesis, not a declaration that every equation below has been empirically validated as a universal law.

---

## 1. Improvement capacity

The central orientation is:

\[
I \propto C_{\mathrm{improve}}
\]

where:

\[
C_{\mathrm{improve}}
=
\text{capacity to convert feedback into increased future viability.}
\]

The recursive chain is:

\[
\text{feedback}
\rightarrow
\text{better representation}
\rightarrow
\text{better adaptive mechanisms}
\rightarrow
\text{greater capacity to improve}
\rightarrow
\text{expanded reachable futures}.
\]

This is not a validated scalar metric of intelligence.

It is the organizing research hypothesis.

---

## 2. Minimal evolutionary basis

\[
\boxed{
D_c \times C_e \times A_c \rightarrow G_{\mathcal V}
}
\]

### \(D_c\): causal depth

Can environmental consequences reach the machinery responsible for future adaptation?

Without it:

\[
E^*\nrightsquigarrow C_{\mathrm{rev}}.
\]

The system may change behavior while the source of future change remains closed.

### \(C_e\): consequence coupling

Do consequences influence which changes survive?

Without it:

\[
\Delta F\neq0
\]

but:

\[
\Delta F_{\mathrm{selected}}
\approx
\text{internally arbitrary variation}.
\]

### \(A_c\): adaptive consolidation

Do successful changes become substrate for future adaptation?

Without it, the system repeatedly solves local failures without improving the machinery available to later cycles.

### \(G_{\mathcal V}\): viable reach expansion

Does the lineage increase the set of future trajectories that remain viable under novelty?

The central distinction is:

\[
\text{surviving one perturbation}
\neq
\text{becoming better able to survive future perturbations}.
\]

---

## 3. Causal permeability

The topological requirement is:

\[
\boxed{
P_C\equiv E^*\rightsquigarrow C_{\mathrm{rev}}
}
\]

where \(C_{\mathrm{rev}}\) is the process governing how future revision rules are generated.

Closed trajectory:

\[
C_{\mathrm{rev},t+1}
=
f(C_{\mathrm{rev},t}).
\]

Open lineage:

\[
C_{\mathrm{rev},t+1}
=
f(C_{\mathrm{rev},t},E_t^*).
\]

Self-modification is therefore insufficient.

The deciding question is whether reality can alter what self-modification means in later cycles.

---

## 4. Recursive change hierarchy

A useful hierarchy is:

### Optimization

Changes candidate solutions.

### Learning

Changes internal state or policy.

### Meta-learning

Changes update rules or solution generators.

### Evolutionary agency

Changes the selection process determining which future adaptive mechanisms survive.

One representation is:

\[
X_{t+1}=F_t(X_t)
\]

\[
F_{t+1}=G_t(F_t)
\]

\[
G_{t+1}=H_t(G_t,\Omega_t).
\]

The critical distinction is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{change of the system}
\neq
\text{change of the source of change}
}
\]

---

## 5. Representation and reachability

For task \(T\), interface or representation \(\rho\), architecture \(A\), and induced computational cost \(\mathcal C\):

\[
\mathcal C(\rho,T)\leq A
\]

is the reachability condition.

Difficulty is not a property of the task alone.

It is relational:

\[
\text{tractability}
=
f(T,\rho,A,\text{resources}).
\]

Representations alter:

- visible distinctions;
- invariants;
- operation cost;
- search geometry;
- reachable solutions.

---

## 6. Representation lifecycle

The recurring representation cycle is:

\[
\text{unknown structure}
\rightarrow
\text{expand}
\rightarrow
\text{extract invariants}
\rightarrow
\text{compress}
\rightarrow
\text{new anomaly}.
\]

The goal is neither maximal complexity nor rigid simplicity.

It is the capacity to metabolize temporary complexity into reusable predictive structure while preserving the possibility that the current representation may later fail.

---

## 7. Contradiction update chain

The preferred update sequence is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Error}
\rightarrow
\text{Attribution}
\rightarrow
\text{Authority revision}
\rightarrow
\text{Mechanism revision}
\rightarrow
\text{Representation revision}
\rightarrow
\text{Expanded viable futures}
\rightarrow
\text{Calibration of update magnitude}
}
\]

Not every contradiction should traverse every layer.

The purpose of attribution is to stop revision at the lowest sufficient causal depth.

---

## 8. Error addressability

Let commitments be \(C_t=(c_1,\ldots,c_n)\) and residuals \(e_t=(e_1,\ldots,e_m)\).

The conceptual correction map is:

\[
J_{ij}
=
\frac{\partial c_{i,t+1}}
{\partial e_{j,t}}.
\]

The desired topology is:

- reachable by valid contradiction;
- sparse enough to preserve unaffected structure;
- deep enough to alter the true failure source;
- proportional to evidence strength;
- itself open to correction.

---

## 9. Identifiability before intervention

For class \(F\), interface \(O\), and target \(L\):

\[
\boxed{
L=\widehat L\circ O
}
\]

is the factorization condition.

Equivalently:

\[
O(f_a)=O(f_b)
\Rightarrow
L(f_a)=L(f_b)
\qquad
\forall f_a,f_b\in F.
\]

The no-bypass hierarchy is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{factorization}
\rightarrow
\text{estimation}
\rightarrow
\text{predictive validity}
\rightarrow
\text{causal intervention}
}
\]

A downstream success cannot repair an upstream failure.

---

## 10. Candidate lineage invariant

The deepest recurring candidate invariant is not a fixed model, architecture, objective, or constitution.

It is a preserved relation:

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal R_{\mathrm{corr}}:
\text{validated environmental consequence}
\rightarrow
\text{proportional improvement of future adaptation}
}
\]

A mechanism may be replaced while this relation survives.

A mechanism may persist while this relation disappears.

---

## Final kernel

The shortest complete process is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Reality}
\rightarrow
\text{Representation}
\rightarrow
\text{Action}
\rightarrow
\text{Consequence}
\rightarrow
\text{Attribution}
\rightarrow
\text{Revision}
\rightarrow
\text{Inheritance}
\rightarrow
\text{expanded future viability}
}
\]

Its verbal compression is:

> Evolution begins when adaptation becomes the substrate of adaptation, and remains grounded only while reality retains the authority to revise that substrate.
