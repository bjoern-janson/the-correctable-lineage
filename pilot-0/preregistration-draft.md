# Pilot 0 Preregistration Draft

## Status

This is a draft checklist of decisions that must be independently reviewed and frozen before recruitment.

It is not a registered protocol.

No hypotheses, thresholds, exclusions, or analyses below should be treated as preregistered until deposited in a timestamped external registry before data collection.

---

## 1. Study title

Pilot 0: Independent Reconstruction and Revision of Epistemic Governance Representations

---

## 2. Study purpose

Primary feasibility question:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Can outsiders instantiate and revise the representations without framework-author arbitration?}
}
\]

Secondary feasibility questions:

1. Which manual ambiguities prevent reliable use?
2. How much translation burden does each representation impose?
3. Do later evidence stages improve behavioral convergence?
4. Can plural adjudicators score consequences without using framework vocabulary?
5. Do method-native augmentations eliminate apparent representational differences?

No comparative-superiority claim is authorized by Pilot 0.

---

## 3. Design

- Between-subject representation assignment.
- Three staged neutral cases.
- Evidence released sequentially.
- Participants complete one response before receiving the next stage.
- Independent adjudication performed before condition-level outcomes are inspected.
- Descriptive feasibility analysis.

## 18. Precommitted interpretation

### Feasible public instrument

Outsiders instantiate and revise records with acceptable malformed rate, consequence quality, and translation burden.

Interpretation:

> Proceed to independently authored comparative study after revising manuals and cases.

### Usable only with training or facilitation

Participants improve after examples or specialist support, but blind reconstruction is unstable.

Interpretation:

> Scope the representation as a trained practice rather than a self-executing public instrument.

### Synthesis or augmentation outcome

Method-native augmentations reproduce the useful distinctions with comparable or lower burden.

Interpretation:

> Treat claim contracts as synthesis, pedagogy, or notation unless later evidence identifies additional value.

### Private-ontology failure

Records require inventor interpretation or divergent structures produce incompatible actions without case-based resolution.

Interpretation:

> Do not proceed to superiority testing. Revise or narrow the framework.

### Evaluation failure

Cases or adjudication cannot support consequence-based scoring without embedding the framework ontology.

Interpretation:

> The evaluation substrate failed; no authority update about the representation is permitted.
